Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install anything or sign up for a subscription?
No. iCFO is a done-for-you service, not software. You send us your data, our AI agents do the work behind the scenes, and a qualified ACCA reviews and signs off every statement to Big-4 caliber standard. There’s nothing to install, log into, or subscribe to — you just receive the finished, audit-ready package.
What is IFRS and who needs it?
IFRS is the global standard for financial statements. You typically need it if you have international investors, are part of a foreign group, or are preparing for an IPO or acquisition. Local standards like German HGB often don’t provide the comparability international stakeholders require.
How long does the process take?
Once we have your complete data (trial balance, general ledger, prior-year statements), our standard turnaround is 5–10 business days for a complete, ACCA-reviewed IFRS package.
Do you replace my Steuerberater?
No. We’re a specialised extension of your finance team. Your Steuerberater continues day-to-day accounting, payroll and local tax filings (e.g. HGB). We take that output and construct the IFRS reporting layer on top.
What data do you need to start?
Typically your current-year trial balance, general ledger detail, prior-year statements (for comparatives), and a short call to understand any complex transactions or accounting policies.
Can you work directly with my auditor?
Absolutely. We build statements to be audit-ready and provide clear local-GAAP-to-IFRS mappings. We answer technical queries from your auditors during their review.
